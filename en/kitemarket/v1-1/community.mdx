## Favorites and saved searches

Favorite an order and manage it in the community area. Each player can retain 256 favorites and 20 searches. Unavailable orders remain removable without appearing as valid merchandise.

Searches preserve type, currency, material, readable text, business source, category, price bounds, single/bundle kind, featured state and sorting. Running a search reads current orders; saved results are not guaranteed stock.

Material-name matching follows the main market. Saving “钻石” still finds and subscribes to diamond procurement without an exact sample. Editing the text updates material matches while preserving other filters.

Material filters accept Chinese names, English material names or namespaced IDs, such as `钻石`, `DIAMOND` and `minecraft:diamond`, and store a normalized material ID. Invalid input leaves the previous filter intact.

Name searches include readable text without color codes, so per-character gradients do not split keywords. Modern items without a custom display name use the public API's `item_name`; custom display names take priority. Search names do not replace business IDs or complete-property matching.

Price bounds are non-negative integer minor units. `123` means 1.23 for a scale-2 currency. Equal integers in different currencies do not represent equal buying power. Switching currency clears the previous minimum and maximum prices; enter new bounds for the selected currency. Other filters are retained.

Subscriptions apply to future matching orders. Validated search edits do not automatically buy, supply or bid.

The favorite/subscription discovery index supports up to 1,024 players. A full index rejects new registrations before saving preferences. Unsubscribed manual saved searches remain available.

## Personal notices

Notices cover matching new orders, terminal favorites, purchases, sales and auction outbids. Same-type events merge by minute, deduplicate replay and retain up to 100 entries.

Notices persist in the market network when players are offline. Disabling personal notices affects future entries without deleting the inbox or asset records.

Login reports actual unread entries. Online polling combines changes into at most one reminder every 15 seconds. Growth in the same-minute notice produces an update without one message per event.

Delivery does not mark notices read. Opening details or explicitly marking all read updates durable status. Chat shows only a few short order references; details retain targets. Failed reads never appear as zero unread.

## Public broadcasts and Discord

Both default off and require administrator configuration.

Public broadcasts consume new orders with independent node cursors and combine them over 60 seconds by currency and type. Amounts from different currencies are not added or compared; arbitrary item names and lore are excluded.

Discord retains message identity for each actual order: create once, update the same message when remaining quantity or highest bid changes, and delete it when the order becomes terminal. Network limits and daily quotas coalesce repeated changes. `allowed_mentions` disables mentions.

Summaries localize order types, materials and currencies, formatting amounts at the currency's precision. Unit prices, whole-bundle prices, starting lot prices and current lot bids have distinct labels. Bundles show their actual item count; auctions show the complete lot quantity. Buy orders without a sample list only public materials and the condition mode. Inspect full conditions in-game.

Messages retain an order alias and omit player identities and original names/lore/NBT, with no executable trade buttons.

`notifications.discord.language` accepts `zh_CN`, `en_US` or the default `auto`. `auto` inherits the server's `language`; when that is also `auto`, it uses Chinese. Currency labels read the selected language's `display-name`. Customize wording under `community.discord` in `lang/community_<language>.yml`.

Discord configuration and its display snapshot are fixed at startup. Restart the server normally after changing them; `/km reload` does not update this configuration.

A timed-out creation, server error or unconfirmed message ID remains UNKNOWN without automatic duplicate creation. An explicitly rejected rate-limited request may retry after its delay. Message delivery failure does not roll back completed game trades.

## Sellers and rankings

Seller profiles and rankings aggregate committed trades separately for each seller and currency. The 1-, 7- and 30-day choices use **UTC calendar days**, including the current day. They are not exact rolling 24-, 168- or 720-hour windows.

Means, volume and net revenue use actual observations. Failed reads and absent data remain distinct. Switching currency changes the scope; it does not create an exchange rate or mixed-currency ranking.

Rankings can switch between net income, completed trade count and quantity sold. Open a seller's statistics and orders from its ranking entry. Seller pages also include completed orders; historical quantities are not current stock, and trading availability follows the order state.

The seller index supports up to 1,024 players. Beyond that limit, exact individual statistics continue while complete rankings become explicitly unavailable. Indexed sellers are not presented as a complete network ranking.

## Public reference prices

Reference prices group complete comparable items in one currency over rolling 24-hour, 7-day and 30-day windows.

| Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Median | Median of committed trade unit prices |
| Mean | Unit price weighted by actual traded quantity |
| Volume | Genuine committed quantity and trade records in the window |

Different properties, bundle compositions or currencies are not forcibly merged. Missing comparable data is not a zero valuation. References are neither executable quotes nor findings of cheating.
