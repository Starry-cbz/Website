## Drafts and confirmation

Choose “Create / edit draft” on `/km` home to open the wizard directly and continue the current draft. Each market and “My orders” list retains the same entrance.

Returning, refreshing or switching interfaces retains unpublished conditions. Published orders must be cancelled and recreated to change them.

Listing, purchase, and bid confirmations retain the real sale/auction item's name, enchantments, and lore. Exact-sample buy orders also show their saved sample.

Use the confirmation text for quantity, total, reserved funds, and tax; display items never become the assets removed or settled.

### Single items, bundles and batches {#v11-lots-and-batch-listings}

A single-item fixed-price sale uses a unit price and can set a minimum purchase quantity. A bundle combines real items into one indivisible lot sold at a total price. Auctions can also use bundles; the winning bid covers their complete contents.

Batch listing creates separate orders, each reviewed and confirmed individually. It is different from a bundle. An unpublished plan exists only in the current session and never resumes automatically after leaving or restarting. Do not resubmit orders that have already succeeded.

### Fees and listing policy {#v11-fees-and-listing-policy}

Confirmations show listing, buyer and seller fees separately, together with asset destinations. Each order retains its fee snapshot. Partial trades allocate fees against cumulative amounts, with the final trade settling the remainder; reloading configuration never recalculates existing orders.

Administrators can restrict worlds, players, items and creative mode, and set permission-based listing quotas, quantities, prices, durations, cooldowns and currencies. A rejected draft remains available with a reason. Final submission rechecks policy without silently changing prices or quantities.

<Note>
All modes forbid self-purchasing, self-fulfillment, and self-bidding. Published prices, conditions, and fees are fixed; cancel and recreate to change them. Administrators cannot directly reverse completed trades.
</Note>

### Listing quantities and maximum values {#listing-quantities-and-maximum-values}

The wizard calculates the maximum from what is actually available and rechecks it when applying a quantity and confirming publication. See [quantity limits and calculations for all three modes](/en/kitemarket/trading/sales#listing-quantities-and-maximum-values).
