## Drafts and confirmation

Choose “Create / edit draft” on `/km` home to open the wizard directly and continue the current draft. Each market and “My orders” list retains the same entrance.

Returning, refreshing or switching interfaces retains unpublished conditions. Published orders must be cancelled and recreated to change them.

Listing, purchase, and bid confirmations retain the real sale/auction item's name, enchantments, and lore. Exact-sample buy orders also show their saved sample.

Use the confirmation text for quantity, total, reserved funds, and tax; display items never become the assets removed or settled.

<Note>
All modes forbid self-purchasing, self-fulfillment, and self-bidding. Published prices, conditions, and fees are fixed; cancel and recreate to change them. Administrators cannot directly reverse completed trades.
</Note>

### Listing quantities and maximum values {#listing-quantities-and-maximum-values}

The wizard calculates the maximum from what is actually available and rechecks it when applying a quantity and confirming publication. See [quantity limits and calculations for all three modes](/en/kitemarket/trading/sales#listing-quantities-and-maximum-values).
