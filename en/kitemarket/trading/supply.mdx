Previewing does not move or modify inventory items. Fulfillment transfers snapshots of the actual selected items instead of generating replacements from the buyer's sample.

## Fulfillment steps {#fulfillment-preview}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Inspect matching items">
    Click **Supply** in a buy order to preview your inventory storage slots.

    Icons retain the real name, enchantments, and original lore, with added match results, rejection reasons, and quantities to take and leave.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Protect items you want to keep">
    Click a matching slot to protect it; click again to include it. Protected items are excluded from this fulfillment.

    Protection belongs only to this preview flow, not a permanent inventory setting; select it again when starting another fulfillment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the fulfillment quantity">
    Set a quantity or use **Maximum available** to recalculate from unprotected matching items and the order's remaining demand.

    Items are selected in inventory-slot order. A shortfall is shown and prevents final confirmation.

    **Refresh** reads the order and inventory again for the latest available quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review items and proceeds">
    Review the quantity from each slot, the trade amount, supplier fees and net income. The procurement owner bears buyer fees, while the supplier bears seller fees; both are shown separately.

    The confirmation also lists source slots and quantities so you can catch a selected tool or collectible before submitting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm fulfillment">
    After final confirmation, the actual inventory properties and quantities are checked again before removal; submitting the order rechecks its revision and remaining demand. Inventory changes reject removal.

    If the order expires or another supplier fills it, use the operation record to check any items already escrowed and inspect claims before starting a new operation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For items that do not match, see [advanced buy orders and conditions](/en/kitemarket/trading/buy-orders). After a trade or item return, open [claims](/en/kitemarket/trading/claims).

## Fulfillment fees and asset destinations {#v11-supply-fees-and-destination}

Publication stores the buy order's fee snapshot. The trade amount is the unit price multiplied by the actual supplied quantity. The procurement owner pays that amount plus buyer fees, while the supplier receives it minus seller fees. Net income enters the supplier's market wallet; the actual supplied items enter the buyer's claims. Listing fees are separate charges at publication.

Partial fulfillment by multiple suppliers allocates fixed and percentage fees against cumulative order turnover. The combined cumulative fees are rounded before subtracting fees already collected, and the final fulfillment settles the remainder. Suppliers are not each charged the whole-order fixed fee, and a fulfillment's seller fee cannot exceed its trade amount.

Preview and final confirmation use the same stored fee snapshot rather than recalculating fees in a theme. Changed order revisions, quantities, inventory or quotes require a fresh preview and confirmation. Changing server fee settings does not rewrite published orders. See [fee snapshots and cumulative allocation](/en/kitemarket/trading/sales#v11-fee-snapshot-and-allocation).
